# Contributing

Thanks for contributing.

This repository is curated rather than exhaustive. Please optimise for relevance and quality, not raw volume.

## What to add

Good additions usually meet several of these criteria:

- directly relevant to the safety, evaluation, control, observability, or governance of multi-agent AI systems — interacting agent populations, the infrastructure they rely on, or their collective behaviour
- technically credible
- documented well enough for a practitioner to use
- actively maintained or historically important
- meaningfully distinct from entries already in the list

## What to avoid

Please avoid submitting entries that are:

- generic AI tooling with no clear safety or control relevance
- single-agent safety content (prompt-injection defence, guardrails, sandboxing, individual-agent benchmarks) with no multi-agent relevance
- duplicate or near-duplicate with existing entries
- primarily marketing pages with little technical detail
- stale, abandoned, or thinly documented unless historically important

## Entry style

Use this format:

- **[Name](link)** - One-sentence description. *Optional metadata*

Keep descriptions:

- concise
- technically precise
- neutral in tone
- focused on why the resource matters for multi-agent AI safety

## Placement

Add entries to the single best-fit section in `README.md`.

If a resource could fit multiple sections, prefer the section where it is most actionable for readers.

## Pull requests

When opening a pull request:

1. Explain what you added, removed, or changed.
2. Say why the resource belongs in this repository.
3. Note any link updates, category moves, or duplicate cleanup.

## Editorial preference

We prefer:

- official documentation, official repos, official benchmark sites, and primary papers
- production-relevant resources over hype
- careful curation over category sprawl
